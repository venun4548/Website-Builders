Enterprise Trust & Security

Bank-Grade Security for Your Web Infrastructure

How Website Builders protects your client data, transactions, and intellectual property.

256-Bit SSL / TLS Encryption

All network traffic between your browser and our platform is encrypted in transit using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols.

  • Strict HTTPS enforcement
  • Automated SSL renewal
  • HSTS preloading headers

PCI-DSS Level 1 Payment Security

We do not store credit card or bank account credentials. All transactions are securely routed through PCI-DSS certified payment gateways (Razorpay/Stripe).

  • Tokenized checkout
  • Digital cryptographic invoice verification
  • Automated instant payment receipts

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Administrator accounts are protected with RFC 6238 Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) verification using Google Authenticator or Authy.

  • Rate-limited login challenge
  • Brute-force protection & session rotation
  • Zero plain-text secret storage

Data Privacy & Continuous Backups

Your project files, revision assets, and client records are stored with role-based access control and persistent cloud backups.

  • Strictly scoped client isolation
  • Non-destructive document versioning
  • GDPR & regional privacy alignment

99.98% High Availability

Hosted on geo-distributed cloud infrastructure with health monitoring, automatic failover, and zero single points of failure.

  • 24/7 uptime monitoring
  • Edge CDN asset acceleration
  • Rapid incident recovery response

Full Audit Logging

Every administrative action, permission change, document signature, and invoice generation is recorded in tamper-resistant audit logs.

  • IP hash & timestamp tracking
  • User action provenance
  • Real-time anomaly detection
