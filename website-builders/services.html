Frequently Asked Questions

Insights into our process, timelines, and deliverables.

How long does a typical project take?

Our commitment to quality means we don't rush. A premium corporate website typically takes 4-6 weeks from initial discovery to global launch. Complex platforms like custom e-commerce or web apps can span 8-12 weeks.

Do you use templates or custom designs?

We are a 100% custom design agency. We do not use pre-built templates or rigid themes. Every pixel is crafted specifically for your brand, ensuring a unique and memorable digital presence.

Who owns the website and code after launch?

You do. Upon final payment and launch, full intellectual property rights, source code, and design assets are transferred to your company. There are no hidden licensing fees.

Do you offer post-launch support?

Absolutely. The launch is just the beginning. We offer dedicated retainer packages for ongoing feature development, security updates, performance monitoring, and conversion rate optimization.
